What is RAG-LLM?

A large language model understands and generates language. RAG connects that model to trusted external knowledge before it answers. Together, they produce responses that are more relevant, explainable and grounded in evidence.

Part one

What is an LLM?

An LLM, or large language model, is an AI system trained to understand and generate human language.

What it does well

It recognises language patterns and turns information into natural, readable responses.

✓
Understands questionsInterprets informal wording and user intent.
✓
Generates clear languageExplains information in a useful form.
✓
Structures answersCreates summaries, steps and recommendations.

What it cannot guarantee alone

It does not automatically check your latest, private or domain-specific information before answering.

!
Trusted evidenceA fluent answer may still be unsupported.
!
Current business knowledgeIt may not know your newest SOPs or records.
!
Correct domain contextSpecialised terms can be misunderstood without sources.
Think of the LLM as an excellent writer. It still needs the correct reference material.
Part two

What is RAG?

RAG means retrieval-augmented generation. It retrieves relevant information and gives it to the LLM before the answer is generated.

RAG is the evidence layer

It connects the user’s question to the knowledge most likely to answer it correctly.

1
SearchFind relevant records, scenarios or documents.
2
SelectChoose the strongest supporting evidence.
3
Provide contextAdd that evidence to the LLM prompt.

Why RAG is useful

The knowledge can be updated without retraining the whole language model.

✓
Uses your own knowledgeConnects the model to private or specialised information.
✓
Improves traceabilityShows what evidence was retrieved.
✓
Supports fresher answersUpdate the knowledge base when information changes.
Think of RAG as a researcher who finds the right source before the writer begins.
Why the connection matters

Why RAG-LLM matters

A model can sound confident even when it does not have the correct source. RAG gives the LLM relevant evidence and makes the answer easier to verify.
